### Project Overview
Designed and built a compact digital scale using a load-cell-based sensing system to achieve accurate and stable weight measurements across a 0–5 kg range.

---

### Technical Highlights & Analysis
* **Machining & CAD Modeling:** Modeled a water-resistant chassis in SolidWorks and fabricated a custom aluminum load cell flexure using a manual lathe and mill while maintaining a critical tolerance of ±0.010".
* **Signal Processing & Firmware:** Integrated an Arduino Nano with an HX711 24-bit ADC for high-resolution signal processing. Coded C++ algorithms to implement multi-point calibration and digital filtering to minimize electrical noise and ensure output stability.
* **System Integration & Validation:** Assembled a functional prototype incorporating internal control electronics, a display, and a user interface. Verified performance through testing, achieving ±1% measurement accuracy with zero deformation under maximum load.
