# Hi, I'm Kyle.

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/engineering.jpg" alt="Kyle Stellmann" style="width: 100%; max-width: 400px; height: auto; margin-bottom: 20px; border-radius: 4px;">

I’m a Junior studying Mechanical Engineering at **Rowan University**, born and raised in Wantage, New Jersey. 

Ever since I can remember, I’ve been fascinated by taking things apart to see how they work and putting them back together better than I found them. To me, Engineering is more than just equations and calculations, it is about the feeling of building something tangible and seeing an idea function in the real world.


---

### What Drives Me

I like taking on challenges that push me out of my comfort zone. Whether I’m spending hours in the machine shop or troubleshooting code on a microcontroller, I thrive on the process of learning through doing.

### Life Beyond Engineering

When I’m not in class or in the campus machine shop:

* I volunteer with **Assistive Technologies**, designing custom tools and controls to help make everyday objects more accessible for people with mobility challenges.
*I work at Liscio’s Bakery to help pay my way through school. It’s fast-paced, keeps my time management sharp, and reminds me of the value of plain old hard work.
* I’m an active member of Rowan’s **Martinson Honors College** and **ASME**.
